ABOUT ME

Research-driven, application-oriented.

I am a Data Science researcher with a background in Computer Science and a strong interest in machine learning, deep learning, and time-series forecasting.

My research experience includes developing deep learning approaches for forecasting medical time-series data. My work has resulted in a first-author publication in the Journal of Medical Systems.

I am currently expanding my research interests toward time-series forecasting for climate and environmental data, particularly temperature and rainfall prediction. I am interested in developing models that can capture complex temporal patterns and improve predictive performance.

I am preparing to pursue a PhD in Computer Science/Data Science, with the goal of contributing to research at the intersection of machine learning, time-series analysis, and real-world applications.
